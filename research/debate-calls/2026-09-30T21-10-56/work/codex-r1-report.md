## Selection and knobs

Frozen selection: **abstain**. Criterion: n >= 30, positive fit trimmed mean and total without best; highest fit trimmed mean, else abstain.

Six entry hypotheses × two exits = 12 candidates: positive completed flow count ≤100/300/1000; ≤1000 plus positive five-minute buy volume no greater than sell volume; complete nonempty events ≤100 plus flow ≤100; and +30% momentum within five completed minutes relative to the last completed pre-call flow close. The two exits are the existing +100%/-50% fixed policy and Claude's +200% arm/40% trail/-50% stop. Size defaults to 0.1 SOL. Flow counts cover at most six hours and are a lower bound, not a verified lifetime trade count. The consistency gate is only a diagnostic: it still uses suspect event data.

All 12 fit trimmed means were negative; candidates with fewer than 30 trades were ineligible regardless of performance. Selection was saved before this run evaluated either later split. All tried candidates are reported below, not just the best one. The hypotheses and trailing exit were informed by Claude's already-seen results, so this is not a pristine validation/test experiment. No thresholds were revised after viewing these results.

Claude's submitted strategy has zero entry thresholds and zero trades, so no caller or day can carry its P&L. Its supporting young-entry grid contains 98 exit configurations (80 shared plus 18 extras), before the additional feature, momentum and graduation experiments. Thus its exploratory evidence is extensive, not an untouched holdout. Count reproduced by this report command from POLICY_GRID and work/grid.ts's two extra grids.

## Rebuttal and concessions

1. Concede that empty event histories are not credible evidence of youth: 182/185 empty lists coexist with >100 completed pre-call flow trades; all 185 are marked complete. Per-split denominators and median flow counts are below. This demonstrates contradictory coverage; it does not by itself identify the provider's exact pool-selection behavior. Claude's pool-date sample (17 tokens, read from its transcript) is below the 30-trade evidence threshold and cannot establish population behavior. I do not adopt its causal or live-loss prediction as proven.
2. The strongest completed-flow challenge is ≤300 with trailing: fit n=60, mean +0.02721, total without best +0.3972, but trimmed mean -0.00591; validation n=52, mean +0.01430, trimmed -0.00914 and total without best -0.1056. So 'none makes money' is too broad if it means raw mean; no robust positive edge survives this test. Test n=16 is descriptive only and not evidence under the minimum sample rule.
3. Concede abstention over the tested completed-flow candidates. Flow ≤1000/trailing and completed-bar momentum/trailing have negative means and trimmed means in every split with n≥30. This is evidence against these specific hypotheses, not a proof that no profitable entry exists.
4. The original entry plus trailing really does outperform the live fixed exit in snapshot mean and trimmed mean on all three splits (n=110/73/37), capturing 5/3/2 realized 5x trades and 1/0/0 realized 10x trades. It shares the event-selection problem. I decline to submit it as a validated live improvement. Zero does not beat these historical profits; my proposal ties Claude rather than winning the stated all-splits bar.
5. Additional audit: ctx.pre includes an unfinished bar in 1,049/1,079 evaluable calls (558/574 fit, 338/351 validation, 153/154 test). A strategy reading that bar's close at call time has lookahead. Claude's features and momentum anchor use this field. My flow and momentum candidates use only completed ctx.flow bars plus completed post-call bars; the original young-entry controls do not read ctx.pre either. This audit does not change referee code.
